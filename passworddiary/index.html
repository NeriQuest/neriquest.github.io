Zero-Knowledge Guarantee

Built for Uncompromising Privacy

We believe your personal credentials belong solely on your hardware—never on external cloud servers or marketing databases.

💾

100% On-Device SQLite

Your entire vault resides strictly inside an encrypted SQLite database stored on your physical Android device. There are zero remote servers, zero cloud backups, and zero tracking endpoints.

🛡️

Hardware Biometrics & PIN

Protected by Android's native BiometricPrompt API (Fingerprint and Face Unlock) backed by an encrypted fallback Master PIN. Passwords never reside in unencrypted RAM when the app is locked.

⏱️

Instant Background Auto-Lock

The instant PasswordDiary is minimized, switched, or backgrounded, the vault locks immediately. Prying eyes cannot access your secrets even if your phone is unlocked.

🚫

Zero Ads & Zero Telemetry

Unlike commercial password managers that monetize through subscriptions or analytics trackers, PasswordDiary is completely ad-free and contains no user profiling libraries.
