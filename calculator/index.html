In standard Western formatting, digits are grouped by thousands (e.g. 100,000 or 1,000,000). The Indian numbering system places the first comma after the hundreds place (3 digits), and all subsequent commas after every 2 digits (e.g. 1,00,000 for one lakh and 1,00,00,000 for one crore).
