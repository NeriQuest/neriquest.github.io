The Origin Story Indie Dev Journey ✏️

How It Started

ExpenseMate didn't begin in a corporate boardroom. It started in a late-night phone note at 4:20 AM, followed by ink on notebook paper, sketching out instant calculations, cash flow graphs, and zero-clutter privacy before writing a single line of Flutter code.

💡 4:20 AM · The First Spark!
Click to Zoom
Phase 01 · Conceptual Spark Phone Notes @ 4:20 AM

"App idea: Expenses calculator"

Born out of frustration with clunky expense apps burdened by full-screen ads and subscriptions. Captured right before sleep: tracking simple everyday essentials like "shawarma & biriyani" and "bus & uber", plus split bills, category limits, and local privacy.

🌯 Shawarma, biriyani 🚌 Bus, uber 👥 Split with friends 🔒 100% Private & Ad-free
Then drawn into notebook wireframes
📐 July 2024 · Ink & Paper
Click to Zoom
Phase 02 · Paper Blueprint Notebook Wireframe · July 2024

Architecting the Flow with Pen & Paper

Every signature feature was sketched out before writing code: the monthly balance curve, the rapid custom numpad (so you don't fight system keyboards), and the live visual category limit bar with the note: "update immediately".

📈 Cash Flow Curve ⌨️ Custom Instant Numpad 🎯 Live Budget Limit Gauge ⚡ "Update Immediately"
The Culmination · v4.1 Today

From Notebook Lines to 120Hz Flutter Reality

Today, ExpenseMate is a full-featured, zero-ad, offline-first personal financial operating system. What started as a 4:20 AM note now features 8 dedicated modules, visual analytics, subcategory hierarchies, biometric locks, and instant CSV/PDF exports.
