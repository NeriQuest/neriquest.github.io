Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about ExpenseMate? Here are the answers.

Is ExpenseMate free to use? ›

Yes! ExpenseMate offers core expense and budget tracking features completely free, without intrusive full-screen ads.

Does ExpenseMate require an internet connection? ›

No. ExpenseMate is built offline-first. All your entries, budgets, and calculation features work anywhere without requiring mobile data or Wi-Fi.

Where is my financial data stored? ›

Your privacy is our priority. Your financial transactions are stored locally on your device storage and never transmitted to third-party ad networks.

Can I export my transactions? ›

Yes, you can easily export your data to CSV format anytime to view in Excel, Google Sheets, or keep a personal backup.
