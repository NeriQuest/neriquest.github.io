01

Introduction & Core Philosophy

Welcome to NeriQuest. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to transparency, data minimization, and user privacy when you use our mobile applications, including ExpenseMate.

ExpenseMate is provided as a free, privacy-first personal expense tracker. Unlike conventional finance applications that force you to create cloud accounts, link online bank credentials, or subject your spending habits to behavioral advertising, ExpenseMate was engineered with an offline-first architecture.

💡
Bottom Line: We do not know who you are, what you spend, how much money you earn, or what categories you create. Your data belongs exclusively to you.
02

Information Collection & Usage

Personally Identifiable Information (PII):

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, government ID, or financial account credentials.

Financial & Transactional Data:

Every transaction, custom category, recurring bill, debt split, and budget limit you record in ExpenseMate is saved into a private, local SQLite database sandboxed within your device's operating system storage. We do not own, maintain, or transmit this data to any remote cloud servers.

Speech & AI Voice Assistant Data:

When utilizing the AI Voice Assistant (e.g. "Spent 250 on groceries from HDFC"), speech-to-text processing is initiated only upon your explicit microphone tap. Spoken audio is processed natively on your device using Android system speech services to extract transaction parameters. No voice recordings or transcripts are uploaded to NeriQuest servers.

03

Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

ExpenseMate requests only the minimal system permissions required to deliver core functionality:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively when you tap the Voice Assistant button to dictate a transaction hands-free. Audio is never captured in the background.
  • Storage / Media Files: Required only when you explicitly trigger a backup to export or import your financial records as Excel (.xlsx) or CSV tables.
  • Biometric Sensors (USE_BIOMETRIC / FINGERPRINT): Enables local device authentication to unlock ExpenseMate. Handled exclusively by the Android OS; we never have access to your biometric samples.
  • Notifications / Alarms: Used locally on your device to remind you of recurring bills, subscription due dates, or daily logging reminders at your configured time.
04

Third-Party Services & Analytics

The application does not include any third-party advertising SDKs or invasive tracking frameworks.

When downloaded from the Google Play Store, standard platform services provided by Google LLC (such as Google Play Services) may process technical device metrics (e.g. OS version, device model, and anonymous crash logs) to facilitate app installation, updates, and stability diagnostics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

05

Data Retention, Backups & Complete Erasure

Because all information is stored locally on your device, you hold complete control over your data lifecycle:

  • Complete Data Erasure: Clearing the app storage in your Android Settings or uninstalling ExpenseMate immediately and permanently erases all databases, settings, and logs from your device.
  • Manual Backups: You can export full unencrypted or encrypted Excel (.xlsx) and CSV backups to your preferred storage provider at any time.
  • Soft-Delete Trash Recovery: Accidentally deleted transactions reside in a local Trash bin where you can restore them or choose "Empty Trash" for immediate permanent erasure.
06

Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Because our apps operate without accounts or cloud storage, no personal information is requested, collected, or stored.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any concerns regarding your child's usage, please contact us immediately.
